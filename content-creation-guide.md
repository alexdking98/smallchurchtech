# AI-Assisted Content Creation Workflow

This guide shows you how to efficiently create new review articles using AI while maintaining your authentic voice and expertise.

---

## 🤖 The Hybrid Approach

**AI handles:** Structure, research, initial draft, SEO optimization  
**YOU handle:** Testing details, personal anecdotes, final editing, authenticity

This is the sweet spot—AI does the heavy lifting, you add the irreplaceable human expertise.

---

## 📝 Creating Your Next Article: Step-by-Step

### Phase 1: Research & Planning (30 minutes)

1. **Choose Your Topic**
   - Look at search volume (Google Trends, Ubersuggest)
   - Pick products you've actually used or can test
   - Focus on specific niches: "best church streaming cameras under $1000"

2. **Competitor Research**
   - Google your target keyword
   - Open top 3-5 results
   - Note what they cover (products, structure, depth)
   - Identify gaps you can fill

3. **Product Selection**
   - Choose 5-8 products to review
   - Ensure range of price points (budget/mid/premium)
   - Must have real testing experience with at least top 3

### Phase 2: AI Draft Generation (30 minutes)

**Use this prompt with Claude:**

```
I need to write a comprehensive review article for my church tech affiliate site.

Topic: [Your topic, e.g., "Best PTZ Cameras for Church Live Streaming Under $1500"]

Products to review:
1. [Product name + key feature]
2. [Product name + key feature]
3. [Product name + key feature]
(etc.)

My testing context:
- Church size: [e.g., 250-seat sanctuary]
- Current setup: [what you currently use]
- Pain points: [what you're trying to solve]

Using the structure from my wireless mic article, create:
1. Article title and meta description
2. Quick picks summary (best overall, best value, best budget)
3. Comparison table
4. Detailed review outlines for each product (I'll fill in testing details)
5. Buying guide section (key decision factors)
6. 5 FAQ questions

Format as HTML using the same CSS classes as the wireless mic article.
```

**What you'll get:**
- Complete article structure
- Placeholder content
- All formatting/CSS in place
- Sections marked for your editing

### Phase 3: Add Your Expertise (2-3 hours)

This is the CRITICAL phase. AI can't do this—only you can.

**For Each Product Review:**

Replace generic AI text with specific details:

❌ **Generic (AI-written):**
> "The camera produces excellent image quality with good low-light performance."

✅ **Specific (Your expertise):**
> "I tested this during our Wednesday evening service when we only use stage lighting (no house lights). At ISO 3200, the image was still clean enough for streaming—our congregation could clearly see facial expressions during worship. Compare that to our old camera which looked like grainy static at anything above ISO 1600."

**What to add:**
- Specific use cases from your church
- Comparison to other products you've used
- Real problems it solved (or didn't solve)
- Installation/setup time ("took me 45 minutes including running cables")
- Volunteer-friendliness ("our 16-year-old tech intern figured it out in 10 minutes")
- Quirks or gotchas ("the menu system is counterintuitive—took 3 tries to find white balance")

### Phase 4: Edit for Voice (1 hour)

**Read the article aloud.** If it doesn't sound like you talking to a friend, rewrite it.

**Voice checklist:**
- [ ] Would I actually say this to another tech director?
- [ ] Did I use overly formal language? (If yes, make it conversational)
- [ ] Did I include at least 2-3 personal anecdotes?
- [ ] Would someone reading this learn something they can't find elsewhere?

**Example transformation:**

❌ **Formal/Generic:**
> "The aforementioned camera system demonstrates exceptional performance characteristics in challenging illumination environments."

✅ **Your Voice:**
> "Here's the thing about this camera—it actually works in our dimly-lit sanctuary. Most cameras turn worship services into grainy messes when we dim the lights for communion. This one doesn't."

### Phase 5: Affiliate Links & Images (30 minutes)

1. **Add affiliate links** (see deployment guide)
2. **Add real product images** (manufacturer sites or your own photos)
3. **Screenshot comparison tables** if helpful
4. **Add pricing** (current as of publication date)

### Phase 6: SEO Polish (20 minutes)

**Quick SEO checklist:**
- [ ] Target keyword in title
- [ ] Target keyword in first paragraph
- [ ] Target keyword in at least one H2
- [ ] Meta description (155 characters, includes keyword)
- [ ] Image alt text describes the product
- [ ] Internal links to 2+ other articles
- [ ] External links to 2-3 authoritative sources

### Phase 7: Publish & Promote (15 minutes)

1. **Upload to site** (add HTML file to `/articles/` folder)
2. **Update homepage** (add to featured articles grid)
3. **Submit to search engines** (Google Search Console)
4. **Share:**
   - Email to your list
   - Post in church tech Facebook groups
   - Share on Twitter/LinkedIn with relevant hashtags
   - Post in /r/churchtech (if genuinely helpful, not spammy)

## ⚡ Quick Article Templates

### Template 1: Product Roundup
**Best for:** "Best [product category] for [audience]"

Structure:
1. Quick picks (3-4 products)
2. Comparison table
3. Detailed reviews (5-8 products)
4. Buying guide
5. FAQ

**Time to create:** 4-5 hours total

---

### Template 2: Head-to-Head Comparison
**Best for:** "[Product A] vs [Product B] for Churches"

Structure:
1. At-a-glance comparison
2. Product A deep dive
3. Product B deep dive
4. Side-by-side testing results
5. Which one to buy (specific scenarios)

**Time to create:** 3-4 hours total

---

### Template 3: How-To Guide
**Best for:** "How to [accomplish goal] on a Church Budget"

Structure:
1. What you'll need (product list)
2. Step-by-step instructions
3. Product recommendations at each step
4. Troubleshooting tips
5. FAQ

**Time to create:** 2-3 hours total

## ✍️ Maintaining Authenticity

**The Golden Rule:** If you didn't test it, don't review it.

**Acceptable:**
- Reviewing products you've used extensively
- Comparing products where you've used 2-3 directly
- Recommending based on peer feedback + research (with disclosure)

**Not Acceptable:**
- Fake reviews of products you've never touched
- Making up testing scenarios
- Copying competitor reviews

**Why this matters:**
1. **Legal:** FTC requires truthful advertising
2. **Ethical:** You're serving church tech directors who trust you
3. **Practical:** Authenticity converts better long-term

**Disclosure Template:**
> "Full transparency: I've personally used and tested [Products A, B, C] in our worship environment. [Products D, E] are based on research and feedback from other tech directors I trust, but I haven't hands-on tested them yet."

## 📊 Content Performance Tracking

**What to measure:**
- Page views (Google Analytics)
- Affiliate clicks (track in affiliate dashboards)
- Email signups per article
- Average time on page
- Bounce rate

**Optimization cycle:**
1. Publish article
2. Wait 30 days for data
3. Identify top performer
4. Analyze what made it successful
5. Update underperformers with those elements
6. Repeat

**Pro tip:** Your best article should be updated every 3 months—refresh testing, update pricing, add new products. This keeps it ranking.

## 🚀 Scaling with AI

**As you grow:**

**Months 1-3:** You + AI (4-6 hours per article)
- You do all testing, AI helps with structure

**Months 4-6:** You + AI + Editor (3-4 hours per article)
- Hire editor for $50-100/article to polish AI drafts
- You focus on testing and final review

**Months 7-12:** You + AI + Editor + Researcher (2-3 hours per article)
- Hire VA to do product research, compile specs
- AI creates structure
- Editor polishes
- You add expertise and approve

**Year 2+:** Team-based
- Partner with other tech directors for testing
- Professional editor on retainer
- VA handles research and admin
- You focus on strategy and expertise

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Over-relying on AI for technical details**
   - AI hallucinates specs
   - Always verify product details on manufacturer site

2. **Ignoring user intent**
   - Don't just stuff keywords
   - Answer the actual question someone has

3. **Publishing without testing**
   - Even if AI writes it beautifully, test before recommending

4. **Forgetting affiliate disclosures**
   - Always disclose, it's legally required

5. **Not updating old content**
   - Outdated articles hurt credibility
   - Set calendar reminder to refresh quarterly
