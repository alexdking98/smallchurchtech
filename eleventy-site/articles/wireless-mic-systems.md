## Buying Guide

### Analog vs Digital

**Digital systems** (like EW-D): Either work perfectly or cut out completely. More resistant to interference, better sound quality, higher cost.

**Analog systems** (like BLX14): Gradual quality degradation at range limits. Less expensive, proven technology.

**Recommendation:** Go digital if budget allows.

## FAQ

**Q: Will these work with my existing sound system?**
A: Yes. All use standard XLR/1/4" outputs.

**Q: How do I avoid WiFi interference?**
A: Use UHF systems (EW-D, BLX14) which operate in different frequency ranges than WiFi.

**Q: Can I add more systems later?**
A: Absolutely. EW-D supports 88 channels, BLX14 supports 12.
