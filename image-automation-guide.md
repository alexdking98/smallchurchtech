## 🔄 Adding Images to New Articles (100% Automated)

When you create new articles, just use this format:

```html
<img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-XXXXX?w=800&h=450&fit=crop" 
     alt="Your description" 
     loading="lazy">
```

### Finding Unsplash Photo IDs:

1. Go to: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/church-audio
2. Find a photo you like
3. Click it
4. Look at URL: `unsplash.com/photos/ABC123xyz`
5. The ID is: `ABC123xyz`
6. Use: `https://images.unsplash.com/photo-ABC123xyz?w=800&h=450&fit=crop`
