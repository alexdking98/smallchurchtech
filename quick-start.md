## ☑️ HOUR 1: DEPLOYMENT (30 minutes)

### Step 1: Choose Hosting (5 min)
- [ ] Go to https://netlify.com
- [ ] Sign up (free account)
- [ ] Verify email

### Step 2: Upload Site (5 min)
- [ ] Download the `church-tech-site` folder to your computer
- [ ] Drag and drop entire folder into Netlify
- [ ] Wait 30 seconds for deployment
- [ ] Click the live URL (looks like: random-name-123.netlify.app)
- [ ] Verify site loads correctly

### Step 3: Custom Domain (10 min) - OPTIONAL
- [ ] Buy domain at Namecheap/GoDaddy (~$10/year)
  - Suggested: churchtechinsider.com, worshiptechreview.com
- [ ] In Netlify: Site settings → Domain management → Add custom domain
- [ ] Update DNS records at your domain registrar
- [ ] Wait 20 minutes for DNS to propagate
- [ ] Test: yourdomain.com should show your site

### Step 4: Quick Customization (10 min)
- [ ] Open `index.html` in text editor
- [ ] Find "Alex Daniels" → Replace with YOUR name
- [ ] Find "your.email@church.org" → Replace with YOUR email
- [ ] Save file
- [ ] Re-upload to Netlify (drag and drop again)

**🎉 Your site is now LIVE!**

## ☑️ HOUR 3: MONETIZATION (1 hour)

### Step 6: Sign Up for Affiliates (20 min)
- [ ] **Amazon Associates:** https://affiliate-program.amazon.com
  - Use your Netlify URL during signup
  - Wait for approval (1-3 days)
- [ ] **Sweetwater:** Email affiliates@sweetwater.com
  - Mention you have a church tech review site
  - Include your site URL
- [ ] **B&H Photo:** https://www.bhphotovideo.com/find/affiliate.jsp
  - Apply with site URL

### Step 7: Add Affiliate Links (20 min)
Once approved (may take 1-3 days):
- [ ] Log into Amazon Associates
- [ ] Search for "Sennheiser EW-D" (or your top pick)
- [ ] Generate product link with your affiliate ID
- [ ] Open article HTML file
- [ ] Find: `https://amazon.com/PLACEHOLDER`
- [ ] Replace with your actual affiliate link
- [ ] Repeat for all products
- [ ] Save and re-upload

### Step 8: Add Real Images (20 min)
- [ ] Go to Canva.com (free account)
- [ ] Create 800x450px designs with product names
- [ ] OR download from manufacturer websites
- [ ] Save as: `wireless-mics-placeholder.jpg` (remove "-placeholder")
- [ ] Upload to `/images/` folder
- [ ] Re-deploy site

## ⚠️ MOST COMMON MISTAKES

Avoid these:
- ❌ Creating content without search demand (use Ubersuggest)
- ❌ Promoting products you haven't tested (kills trust)
- ❌ Ignoring email list (your most valuable asset)
- ❌ Giving up after 1-2 months (this takes 6-12 months)
- ❌ Copying competitor content (write original, based on experience)
- ❌ Not updating old content (Google rewards fresh content)
