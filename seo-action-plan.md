#### 5. Add ALT Text to All Images
**When you upload product images:**

❌ Bad: `<img src="wireless-mic.jpg">`  
✅ Good: `<img src="wireless-mic.jpg" alt="Sennheiser EW-D wireless microphone system for churches">`

**Formula:** `[Brand] [Product] [Purpose/Context]`

Examples:
- "Shure BLX14 wireless lavalier mic system in church sanctuary"
- "Audio-Technica ATW wireless handheld microphone for worship leader"

**Why:** Google can't "see" images, only read ALT text. This helps you rank in Image Search.

### MONTH 1 (Ongoing Optimization)

#### 9. Optimize for Featured Snippets
**Featured Snippet = "Position 0" - the box at top of Google results**

**How to win snippets:**
- Answer specific questions directly
- Use H2 headers as questions: "What wireless mic is best for small churches?"
- Provide concise answers (40-60 words) right after the question
- Use lists, tables, and step-by-step formats

**Example in your article:**
```html

<p>The Sennheiser EW-D is the best wireless mic system for churches under $500. It offers exceptional reliability with zero dropouts in our 6-week test, 12-hour battery life, and professional audio quality that rivals systems 2x the price.</p>
```

## 🚨 Common SEO Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ DON'T:
1. **Keyword stuff** - Don't repeat "church wireless mic" 50 times
2. **Copy content** - Don't plagiarize or use AI content without heavy editing
3. **Ignore mobile** - Your site is already mobile-responsive, keep it that way
4. **Skip Google Search Console** - This is mandatory
5. **Write for robots** - Write for humans, optimize for robots
6. **Expect instant results** - SEO takes 3-6 months minimum
7. **Buy backlinks** - Google penalizes this
8. **Use black hat tactics** - Shortcuts get you banned

### ✅ DO:
1. **Write helpful content** - Actually solve problems
2. **Update regularly** - Fresh content ranks better
3. **Build real relationships** - Get backlinks from real interactions
4. **Track everything** - Use Search Console and Analytics
5. **Be patient** - Consistency beats intensity
6. **Focus on user experience** - Fast, clean, helpful = good SEO
7. **Answer questions** - People search with questions, answer them
